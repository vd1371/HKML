## Programme:

Gautier Marti - My recent attempts at using GANs for simulating realistic stocks returns
[Gautier Marti](http://marti.ai/)

Abstract

I will share my experience on using GANs for generating something else than natural images, in that case correlation matrices of stocks. I can see a few applications such as more valid comparisons of portfolio construction methods (e.g. MV vs. risk parity vs. de Prado’s HRP vs. HERC).
