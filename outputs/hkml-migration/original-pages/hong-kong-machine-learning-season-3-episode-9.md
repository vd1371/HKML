## Talk 2: Detecting Financial Frauds in Practice

Jendrik Jördening, CTO of Nooxit
[Jendrik Jördening](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jendrik/)
[Nooxit](https://www.nooxit.com/)

This talk is a follow-up on the presentation by Marco Schreyer.​

It presents the practical side of Marco’s research on Detecting Financial Frauds using Deep Learning.

Nooxit builds and sells End-to-End Machine Learning Software to amplify business processes.

Nooxit is working on the vision of a digital, automated shared service center as a service in the cloud. Nooxit develops various software applications that automate specific standard processes using Machine Learning. This portfolio is offered on the market as Nooxer Business Suite and is a plug-and-play solution for ERP systems to integrate Artificial Intelligence into business processes.
