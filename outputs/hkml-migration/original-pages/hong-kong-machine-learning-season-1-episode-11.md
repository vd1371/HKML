## Programme:

David Zhu, CTO at Clover International
[David Zhu](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dazhu/?originalSubdomain=hk)

David presented the data and machine learning efforts at Clover Health. They are hiring.

Benoit Goujon from Thales - How to help metro operators improve passenger experience using machine learning?
[Benoit Goujon](https://www.linkedin.com/in/beno%C3%AEt-goujon/)
[Thales](https://www.thalesgroup.com/en)

Benoit presented his R&D work at Thales. He uses various time series forecasting models to predict the number of passengers in the train, and related quantities. His future work will incorporate the topology of the metro network into account so that combined with entries/exit, he obtains a better estimation.

His slides can be found there.
