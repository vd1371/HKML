## Programme:

Talk 1: Machine learning summary in 30 minutes

Speaker: Richard Xu

Abstract: “Posterior inference”, “conjugate functions”, “neural tangent kernels”… We are overwhelmed by the growing number of buzzwords we see in the academic machine learning literature. In this talk, I am tasked with a challenge to try to summarize all the common and niche topics (that I am familiar with) in 30 minutes, hopefully to provide some clarity to audience.
