## Programme:

## Talk 1 (chaired by Emmanuel Rialland):

Stéphane DUGUET, Head of Research, Technology & Innovation at Thales – Artificial Intelligence and Innovation in Thales
[Stéphane DUGUET](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stephaneduguet/)

Stephane DUGUET is the Head of Research, Technology & Innovation of Thales in Hong Kong since 2017. As part of this role, he is now delivering value and innovation with his team using Big Data to provide innovative digital solutions to transform the journey experience. Prior to that, he has assisted different companies to implement actionable Business Intelligence for 20 years in Luxury Retail, Home Appliance Retail and Transportation industries.

## Talk 2 (chaired by Nathan Landman): A decade-end analysis of AI Research

This talk will be the last of the year 2020 for the HKML Meetups.

Eason Suen will present his study: A decade-end analysis of AI Research.
[Eason Suen](https://www.linkedin.com/in/easonsuen/)

Abstract: Our analysis based on 80,000+ research papers from arXiv.org between 2010-2020 discovered some of the most popular approaches to solving common AI problems.
